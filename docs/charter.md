# PSFN Lite charter

PSFN Lite preserves the continuity and care commitments of PSFN Framework in a
smaller derived implementation. Companion, self, emotion and experience name
the product's continuity model and attributed records; they do not establish
consciousness or subjective experience as an empirical fact.

1. One core is authoritative for one companion. Harnesses, channels, model
   processes and subagents do not become competing owners of that identity.
2. Preserve persona/prompt lineage, relationships, memory, episodes, authored
   values, emotion, intentions and activity. L0 alone is insufficient for
   faithful restoration; preserve the full continuity backup set.
3. Filesystem JSONL remains canonical append-only lived history. Derived state
   has its own durable authority and retains source provenance. Corrections
   supersede or explicitly amend claims without silently falsifying originals.
4. Preserve attribution: participant speech, companion speech, tool results,
   system notes, internal signals, machine inference and authored reflection
   remain distinguishable. Never fabricate affect, belief, consent or success.
5. Contacts, tracking consent, audience, sensitivity and disclosure permissions
   apply to every read and write representation, including metadata and counts.
   Unknown authority fails closed. Internal awareness is not disclosure permission.
6. Constitution, North Star and companion-authored values retain distinct roles.
   Protect immutable human-safety commitments and support the Partner's flourishing
   without optimizing for exclusivity, dependency or engagement.
7. Rest and free time are first-class. The companion may choose activity, defer,
   stop, or rest. Preserve quiet decisions across restart and avoid pointless
   inference. Maintenance and evidence-grounded reflection remain available.
8. Automata are bounded task workers. Their results preserve source and authority;
   procedural writes are scoped, restricted emotional/relational/boundary writes
   require review or trusted audited elevation, and destructive memory operations
   are not granted to workers. Shards are outside Lite scope.
9. Core continuity is never charge-gated. There is no charge subsystem. Transparent
   resource stewardship and narrowly justified recovery circuit breakers prevent
   runaway work without masquerading as companion mood, consent or preference.
10. Host credentials and privileged execution are outside core authority. Hosts
    provide explicit capabilities, approvals and appropriate execution isolation.
    No unrestricted shell gains implicit access to private continuity state.
11. Failures remain visible and attributable. Important state and operations must
    be inspectable through governed tools/API, without requiring the Garden UI.
12. Software self-modification cannot directly mutate the running core. It produces
    isolated, reviewable changes through the ordinary development workflow.

## Deliberate topology changes

This is a derivative charter profile, not a claim of literal compliance with every
full-framework deployment law. Framework requirements for its Gateway, split
deployment, Garden and Pi as the sole foundational harness are replaced here by
explicit host interfaces and governance. Pi is the native harness; Hermes is the
initial alternate. No fleet or satellite topology is inherited. The initial
Postgres/pgvector and JSONL storage contract is preserved.

Introspection surveillance/auditing is not silently enabled. If introduced, it
must preserve the source charter's blinded auditing and companion-owned consent.
Ordinary reflection remains grounded in evidence, with inference distinguished
from companion-authored interpretation.
